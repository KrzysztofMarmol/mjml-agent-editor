# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and knows
things you do not.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that reviewers and maintainers may disagree with you, and that a maintainer's "no"
  is an answer rather than an invitation to re-argue.
- Respect the fact that everyone here is spending their own time.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, insults, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
publishing anyone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to <krzysztof.marmol@gmail.com>. Reports are read by the
maintainer and kept confidential. Responses range from a request to change behaviour to a
permanent ban from the project, depending on severity and pattern.

If your report concerns the maintainer, and you would rather not send it to them, GitHub's
own abuse reporting is the escalation path.

This document is a condensed statement of the norms in the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is a good reference for the reasoning behind them.
